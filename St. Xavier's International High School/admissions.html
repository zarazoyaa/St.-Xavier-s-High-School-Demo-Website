Admission Process

Our admission process is designed to be simple, transparent and welcoming for families.

01

Enquiry

Contact the school or submit an enquiry to learn about available classes and admission details.

02

Application

Complete the admission application form and provide the required student information.

03

Interaction

Attend the interaction or assessment process, if applicable to the class.

04

Confirmation

Complete the required formalities after receiving confirmation from the admission office.

Be Prepared

Documents Required

Families may be asked to provide the following documents during the admission process:

  • Student's birth certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Previous academic records, if applicable
  • Transfer certificate, if applicable
  • Parent or guardian identity proof
  • Address proof

Final document requirements may vary by class.

Important Information

Admission Guidelines

Eligibility

Admission is subject to age criteria, class availability and the school's admission guidelines.

School Visit

Parents may contact the admission office to arrange a campus visit and learn more about the school.

Assistance

Our admission team can guide families through the application and documentation process.
