ADMISSION PROCESS

Five steps to getting started.

A simple guide to the typical admission journey.

01

Visit & Counselling

Connect with the school, explore the campus and learn about available classes.

02

Application Submission

Complete the admission application with accurate student and parent information.

03

Document Verification

Provide the necessary certificates and supporting documents for verification.

04

Interaction or Assessment

An age-appropriate interaction or assessment may be conducted if required by school policy.

05

Admission Confirmation

The school communicates the outcome and the next steps for completing enrolment.

DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Documents to keep ready.

  • Birth certificate copy
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Parent or guardian identity proof
  • Address proof
  • Previous academic report card, if applicable
  • Transfer certificate, where required
  • Medical information, if requested

This is an indicative checklist. Confirm the final requirements with the school office.

AGE ELIGIBILITY

Indicative Age Guide

Age guidance adapted from the reference website, calculated as on 31 March of the academic year.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Before you apply.

  • Admission may depend on available seats.
  • Documents must contain accurate information.
  • School fees and deadlines should be confirmed with the office.
  • Transport and uniform arrangements should be discussed separately.
SCHOOL EXPECTATIONS

Growing with responsibility.

  • Regular attendance and punctuality.
  • Respectful behaviour towards teachers and peers.
  • Appropriate school uniform.
  • Parent participation in school communication.

Confirm official school rules before publishing them as policy.
