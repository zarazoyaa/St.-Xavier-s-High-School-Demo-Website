OUR STORY

Education That Shapes Character.

St. Xavier's School is envisioned as a nurturing learning community where education goes beyond textbooks and examinations.

We believe every child has unique abilities and deserves an environment that encourages curiosity, independent thinking, creativity and confidence.

Our approach combines academic learning, practical experiences, sports and cultural activities to support the all-round development of every student.
